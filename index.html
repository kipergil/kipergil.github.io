About me

I'm a Software Architect based in Hertfordshire / London with 20+ years designing and shipping event-driven .NET systems on Azure at enterprise scale — platforms serving 20M+ requests and ingesting 200M+ events per day, and re-architecture work that cut a client's cloud bill by ~£50K a month.

I hold six Microsoft Azure certifications (two Expert-level) and work deeply with Clean Architecture, CQRS/mediator and outbox-based integration.
